Thermal Stability 
============================================================

 To assess thermal stability during extended
multi-camera recording, host CPU/GPU temperatures were logged
throughout a 24-hour session.

Columns: Date, Time, Core Temperatures (avg) [C], GPU Temperature [C]
Source: HWiNFO log, filtered to the Box19 recording window

Session details:
  Window: 2026-07-03 08:39:55 to 2026-07-04 08:39:55 (24 hours)
  Samples: 42,943 (~2 sec intervals)
  Host CPU: Intel Core Ultra 7 265F
  Host GPU: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5060

Summary:
  CPU core temp (C): mean 50.5, min 42.0, max 63.0
  GPU temp (C): mean 36.4, min 34.7, max 38.4

No thermal throttling or shutdown events were observed.
